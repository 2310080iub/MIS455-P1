Fun Things to Do in the Hills

If you want to do something more active than just sitting on a beach, traveling to the Chittagong Hill Tracts is the best choice. Taking a bumpy open-top 'Chander Gari' ride along winding mountain roads with your friends is an experience you won't forget.

The fresh mountain breeze, bamboo chicken cooked over firewood, and seeing local tribal lifestyles makes hill trips very special. Below are two of my absolute favorite outdoor activities shown in a flexbox layout.

Cloud Watching in Sajek Valley

In Sajek Valley, you wake up early in the morning and see thick white clouds floating right outside your wooden resort balcony. Sitting there with a cup of hot tea while watching the sun rise is magical.

Boating on Kaptai Lake

Renting a small trawler boat on Kaptai Lake and cruising between small green islands is super relaxing. You can stop by floating restaurants, take a dip in cool water, and visit Shuvolong waterfall.

Hill Tracts Travel Info:

Before planning your trip, you can check hill district guidelines on the Chittagong Hill Tracts Board or visit the Bangladesh Tourist Police Portal.
