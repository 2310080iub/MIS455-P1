Top Places You Must Visit

If you are planning your next holiday inside Bangladesh, there are two classic places that almost every Bangladeshi loves to visit at least once: Cox's Bazar and the Sundarbans. Both destinations give you completely different vibes, from the sandy ocean waves to the wild mangrove forests.

Getting to these places has become much easier with newer highways and trains. Here is a quick look at why these two spots are so popular, arranged neatly in a grid below.

Cox's Bazar Sea Beach

Cox's Bazar has the longest unbroken sea beach in the world. Walking along the sandy beach in the evening, watching the sunset, and eating fried rupchanda fish from local stalls is something everyone enjoys.

The Sundarbans Mangrove

The Sundarbans is a massive mangrove forest and home to our Royal Bengal Tiger. Taking a 3-day boat tour through the narrow quiet canals while spotting deer and birds is a truly peaceful adventure.
