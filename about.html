About Me and This Website

Hi there! I created this simple website as part of my MIS 455 course assignment to practice basic HTML and CSS. I decided to make it about traveling in Bangladesh because I really love exploring different parts of our country whenever I get some free time between university semesters.

Through this assignment, I learned how to connect multiple web pages using a navigation bar and how to style things using basic CSS like fonts, colors, and margins. I also wanted to remind everyone who travels to keep our tourist spots clean, respect the local people, and avoid throwing plastic bottles or chips packets into rivers and hills.

Helpful Resources:

If you want to read more about our country's heritage, check out the National Web Portal of Bangladesh and the Department of Archaeology.
