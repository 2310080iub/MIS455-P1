Welcome to My Bangladesh Travel Guide

Welcome to my website! I put this guide together to share some of my favorite travel spots across Bangladesh. Our country is full of greenery, calm rivers, and amazing places to visit. Whether you want to relax by the sea in Cox's Bazar or wander through the tea gardens in Sylhet, there is always something new to see.

Why Traveling Around Bangladesh is Fun

What I love most about traveling inside Bangladesh is how simple and budget-friendly it is. You can easily take a train or bus on a Thursday night after classes or work, spend the weekend in the hills or by the beach with friends, and come back feeling refreshed. Plus, the local food like fresh river fish, vuna khichuri, and sweet tea makes every trip worthwhile!

Official Bangladesh Tourism Sites:

Check out the Bangladesh Tourism Board and Bangladesh Parjatan Corporation for official travel notices and motel bookings.
